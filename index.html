What is PhishGuard?

PhishGuard is a free, educational URL phishing detection project created by Abhinav Kumar Mishra. It helps users inspect a website address for common phishing indicators before opening a link.

The scanner checks URL characteristics such as suspicious wording, brand impersonation, unusual hostname patterns, IP-based addresses and other configured warning signals. The v4 ML candidate was independently evaluated on PhreshPhish and failed its false-positive gate (93.06% false-positive rate on the strict external cohort). ML inference remains disabled; live scans use explainable URL heuristics only. Neither a low score nor a suspicious score is a definitive safety verdict.

Why use a URL checker?

Phishing links can imitate trusted websites to trick people into sharing passwords, financial details or personal information. Checking an unfamiliar URL is one useful precaution—but it should never replace careful browsing habits.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

How does PhishGuard check a URL?

It analyzes the submitted URL for configured warning signals, including URL structure and suspicious hostname patterns, then displays a heuristic risk score and the reasons behind it.

Does a low-risk result mean a website is safe?

No. A low-risk result does not guarantee safety. New phishing websites and sophisticated scams may not trigger the checks. Verify the website address independently and never share sensitive information on an unfamiliar site.

Does PhishGuard open the link I submit?

No. The current scanner analyzes the URL string and does not visit or fetch the submitted website.

Is PhishGuard a live threat-intelligence service?

No. It is an educational heuristic scanner, not a guarantee, antivirus product or live threat-intelligence feed.

!
Security note

PhishGuard provides educational risk indicators, not a definitive safety verdict. A low score does not prove a website is safe. When in doubt, don't enter passwords or payment details.
